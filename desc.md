MochaShield

A simulation dashboard for managing flash-crash risk in leveraged trading platforms.

MochaShield monitors market stress, liquidity, leverage, liquidation exposure, and price-feed risk, then demonstrates automated damage-control protocols to reduce simulated cascade effects.

Features
- Flash-crash simulation
- Real-time risk scoring
- Adjustable market-risk parameters
- Automated damage-control protocols
- Liquidation cascade visualization
- Price-feed anomaly detection
- Before/after protection comparison

> Note: MochaShield is a hackathon prototype using simulated data and does not execute real trades or provide financial advice.
